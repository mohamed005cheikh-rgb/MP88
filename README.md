# MP88 · Vintage Vinyl Player

A nostalgic, aesthetic, and fully interactive vinyl record player built for the web.  
Drop your own music files (MP3, WAV, FLAC) and experience a visually immersive audio journey.

**100% Client-Side.** No servers. Your music stays on your device.

---

### ✨ Why this player?
- Music deserves a beautiful interface, not just a plain progress bar.
- Relive the analog feeling of a vinyl record in the digital age.
- A perfect tool for mood setting, study sessions, or simply enjoying your local music collection.

---

### 🔧 Key Features
- **💿 Interactive Vinyl:** The record spins, the tone arm moves, and the label displays the current track.
- **🎨 4 Immersive Themes:** Switch between Dark, Light, Ocean, and Sunset to match your mood.
- **🎵 Drag & Drop Upload:** Drop your audio files (MP3, WAV, FLAC) directly into the player.
- **📋 Dynamic Playlist:** Automatically builds a playlist of all uploaded tracks.
- **🎛️ Full Playback Controls:** Play, Pause, Previous, Next, Shuffle, and Loop/Repeat.
- **⏪ Interactive Seek Bar:** Click anywhere on the progress bar to skip to a specific part of the song.
- **💾 Theme Memory:** Your favorite theme is saved automatically in your browser's local storage.

---

### 🚀 Quick Start
1. Open the player.
2. Click the **"Browse"** area or drag-and-drop your audio files.
3. The record will start spinning and the music will play.
4. Use the controls to navigate your playlist.
